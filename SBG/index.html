ONE LAST THING...

To my Baal ♡

Happy Birthday, Sneha.

I don't think a normal "Happy Birthday" message was enough this time, which is probably why you're sitting here looking at a website that I spent way too much time making.

But I wanted to make something that was actually ours. Something with our pictures, our memories, our stupid jokes, and all the little things that remind me of you.

We've known each other since September 2024, and when I look back at everything since then, I genuinely feel lucky that we met.

A lot has happened in that time. There have been happy days, stressful days, arguments, laughs, completely random conversations and probably way too many moments where neither of us knew what we were doing.

But through all of it, you've been there.

And that's one of the things I appreciate most about you. You've been there for me through every type of situation, even when I wasn't necessarily at my best.

You've listened to me, supported me, encouraged me and somehow managed to make certain difficult days feel a little easier.

I don't always say it properly, but I notice it. And I appreciate it more than you probably realise.

I love our big memories, but I also love the completely ordinary ones.

Us meeting.
The random conversations.
The stupid jokes.
It's all those little things that made "us" what we are.

On your birthday, I just want you to know how important you are to me.

I hope this year brings you closer to every goal you have for yourself. I hope you get opportunities that excite you, people who appreciate you, places that make you happy and moments that remind you how much life still has waiting for you.

And whenever things don't go exactly according to plan, I hope you remember that you don't have to figure everything out alone.

I'll still be here.

Annoying you.
Supporting you.
Making terrible jokes.
And apparently learning some sense of our relationship whenever required. 😂

Happy Birthday, Baal. ♡

I'm really glad I found you.

And I'm even more glad that I get to call you mine.

With love,
Sarthak ♡
